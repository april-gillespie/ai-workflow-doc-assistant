# LinkedIn Project Summary

## Project Title

AI Workflow Portfolio: Enterprise Adoption, Evaluation, and Human-Reviewed Automation

## Short Project Description

A public portfolio of AI-assisted workflow design examples showing how unstructured inputs such as customer feedback, enterprise requests, transcripts, screenshots, notes, observations, and documents can be converted into structured, human-reviewed outputs.

The project focuses on practical AI solutions: adoption-oriented workflow design, hybrid automation and LLM architecture, reusable process patterns, prompt design, evaluation, privacy-aware documentation, uncertainty handling, and human-in-the-loop validation.

## LinkedIn-Safe Summary

I started building a public portfolio of AI-assisted workflow design examples.

The focus is practical: turning messy inputs such as customer feedback, enterprise intake requests, transcripts, screenshots, notes, observations, and documents into structured outputs that support human decisions and fit the way people actually work.

Current examples include a sanitized enterprise customer-feedback capture workflow, expert-governed AI evaluation, enterprise intake and triage, multimodal decision support, voice-to-agent automation, and reusable prompt patterns.

The portfolio is sanitized for public sharing. When an example is inspired by a real situation, the public artifacts use generalized workflows and synthetic or fictionalized materials rather than private source content.

## Positioning Notes

Use this project to signal AI workflow design, enterprise adoption thinking, and practical solution judgment. The strongest framing is not "I use AI tools." The stronger framing is:

> I design repeatable workflows that use AI to convert messy real-world inputs into structured, reviewed outputs that people can trust and will actually use.

A second strong framing is:

> I separate deterministic automation from language and judgment tasks, then design the human-review boundary around operational risk and source-of-truth integrity.

This aligns with AI Solutions Engineer, AI Deployment Engineer, Customer Engineer, Solutions Architect, Technical Marketing, Technical Enablement, and workflow modernization roles.

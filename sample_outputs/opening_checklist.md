Opening Checklist

Before Opening

* Unlock front door
* Disable alarm
* Turn on lights
* Start music
* Turn on espresso machines
* Turn on grinders
* Start POS system

Inventory

* Brew regular coffee
* Brew decaf coffee
* Check pastry inventory
* Restock pastries
* Fill ice bins
* Stock milk
* Stock syrups
* Stock cups, lids, and napkins

Operations

* Verify cash drawer balance
* Review catering orders
* Review mobile orders
* Complete opening cleaning check

Ready for Customers

* Confirm store ready by 6:00 AM

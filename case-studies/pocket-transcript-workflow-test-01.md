# Pocket Transcript Workflow Test 01

**Date:** July 1, 2026

## Objective
Evaluate Pocket as a transcript capture tool for professional workflows.

## Findings
- Positive first impression for quick voice capture and transcript generation.
- Primary limitation: cannot conveniently support separate work and personal phone workflows.
- Professional requirement: work transcripts should remain isolated on a work device.
- Current workflow makes that separation difficult.

## Assessment
Pocket appears well suited for personal knowledge capture, brainstorming, and lightweight documentation. Its suitability for professional use depends on whether work and personal transcript storage can be cleanly separated.

- Workflow analysis
- Data privacy considerations
- AI-assisted documentation
- Technical communication

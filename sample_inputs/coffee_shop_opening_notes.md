Scenario: Coffee Shop Opening Shift

Barista arrives at 5:30 AM.

Unlock front door and disable alarm.

Turn on lights, music, espresso machines, grinders, and POS system.

Brew regular coffee and decaf.

Check pastry inventory and restock if needed.

Fill ice bins.

Stock milk, syrups, cups, lids, and napkins.

Verify cash drawer balance.

Review scheduled catering and mobile orders.

Complete opening cleaning check.

Store opens to customers at 6:00 AM.

Common issues:

* Low milk inventory
* Missing pastries
* Espresso machine not warmed up
* Cash drawer discrepancy
* Mobile order printer offline
